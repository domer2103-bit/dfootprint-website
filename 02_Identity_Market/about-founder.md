## Background

No formal technical background. My day job is operating a CNC folding machine, working late shifts (4:30pm–1am). I've spent the last while learning AI on my own through YouTube — self-taught, passionate about the subject, and I feel ready to turn that into a side hustle.

I already have hosting on Hostinger and run an n8n instance on a VPS there, so I have basic infrastructure in place.

My day runs around my son Leo: wake up, get him ready for school (starts 9am), then I have about 3.5 hours before pickup — time I use for the pool, shopping, and errands. After school we spend time together, then I head to my full-time job.

## Strengths

- Good with people — finding common ground, listening more than talking
- Non-judgmental, empathetic, understanding
- Genuine love of learning
- Enjoys physical activity
- Family-oriented — values time with my son

## Long-term Goals

Start small and get this working. First goal is to match my full-time income through the side hustle. Longer term, I'd like to leave the CNC job and do this full-time — not trading time for money, but building something where I can work 4-5 focused hours a day while Leo is at school, then be fully present with him after school, working from home.

## How I Like to Work

- Solo, self-directed
- Realistically 2-3 focused hours a day given my schedule
- Already comfortable with Hostinger hosting and n8n for automation/workflows

## Business Philosophy

- Teach people how to use AI better, not just sell them tools
- Avoid chasing every new AI hype cycle
- Focus on Claude, Claude Cowork, and Claude Code as the core toolkit
- Stay on top of AI news generally, but keep the offering focused and practical rather than trend-chasing

## Kinds of Clients I Want

Not fussy — open to finding the right fit as I go. Recommended starting niche: non-technical small business owners and solopreneurs who feel overwhelmed by AI — trades people, local service businesses, solo consultants, content creators. They don't need a developer; they need someone who can translate AI into plain language and set up practical workflows (a natural fit with my n8n + Claude skill set). Narrow further once I've worked with a few clients and see what I enjoy most.
