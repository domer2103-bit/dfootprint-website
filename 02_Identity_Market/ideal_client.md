# Ideal Client

> **Layer:** Identity & Market | **Purpose:** Filter/criteria for who Digital Footprint should work with | **Status:** Active — source of truth | **Owner:** Founder | **Version:** 1.1

## Who They Are

Non-technical small business owners and solopreneurs who feel overwhelmed by AI. Trades people, local service businesses, solo consultants, content creators. UK-based, in line with the "UK's best AI implementation business" goal.

## Firmographics

- 5–50 employees
- Owner still involved in daily operations
- Uses email every day
- Has repetitive admin
- Open to technology
- Wants growth
- Doesn't have an IT department

## What They're Struggling With

Repetitive manual work eating their time. Missed leads or slow response to enquiries. Admin that keeps them working late instead of with family or growing the business. They know AI could probably help but don't know where to start, and are put off by hype and jargon.

## What They're Not

Not developers, not technical, not looking to "buy AI" for its own sake. Not chasing the newest shiny tool. Not expecting a magic fix — willing to go through a proper diagnosis first.

## Why They're a Fit

They need someone who can translate AI into plain language and build practical, working solutions — not a technical sales pitch. This matches the founder's own strengths: explaining things simply, empathy, patience, and a non-technical starting point that makes it easy to relate to non-technical clients.

## How They Find Us

Content and positioning built around the "AI Prescription" idea — diagnose the pain, then prescribe the fix — rather than technical AI marketing. Attracted by plain-spoken, no-hype content rather than buzzword-heavy agency messaging.

## Who to Avoid

Clients chasing hype or wanting the newest AI tool for its own sake, without a real problem to solve. Clients wanting a quick fix with no willingness to go through the diagnosis step. Enterprise clients needing large technical teams or heavy compliance overhead — not the current stage of the business.

## Notes

This is a starting point, not a fixed rule. Refine based on real client experience — which types of engagements are most enjoyable, most profitable, and best suited to a 2-3 hour-a-day solo operation.
