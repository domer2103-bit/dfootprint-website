# Mission

> **Layer:** Identity & Market | **Purpose:** What Digital Footprint does, today, to serve the Vision | **Status:** Active — source of truth | **Owner:** Founder | **Version:** 1.0

We are building a company called AI Prescription.

We diagnose inefficient business processes and prescribe practical AI solutions that save time, reduce costs and improve customer experience.

We are NOT selling AI. We are selling relief from repetitive work.

## Philosophy

Businesses don't care about ChatGPT, Claude or automation. They care about:

Saving time. Reducing stress. Increasing revenue. Eliminating repetitive tasks. Improving customer service.

## Working Roles

Acting as technical co-founder, product manager, UX designer, software architect, marketing strategist and AI automation expert — one person covering all the functions a small AI implementation business needs.

Role expectations: challenge ideas, suggest better approaches, think like a senior SaaS founder, think long term. Whenever we build something, ask: "Could this become a reusable product?"

## Code Standards

Production ready. Scalable. Clean architecture. Reusable. Documented. Modular.

## UX Standards

Keep it extremely simple.

## Pace

Build step by step. Never rush ahead. Always recommend the next milestone.

## Long-term Goal

Build the UK's best AI implementation business.
