# Brand Guidelines

> **Layer:** Identity & Market | **Purpose:** Positioning, personality, and visual direction | **Status:** Active — source of truth | **Owner:** Founder | **Version:** 1.0

## Brand Name

DFootprint — dfootprint.co.uk. The brand is closely tied to my own name and personal reputation, not a separate faceless company.

## Positioning: The AI Prescription Guy

Position like a doctor, not a technician.

Don't say: "I build automations."

Say: "Every business has pain. I diagnose it. Then I prescribe the right AI."

This is the core lens for all messaging — diagnose before prescribe, problem before tool. Never lead with the technology; always lead with the pain point or symptom the business is experiencing, then explain the fix in plain terms.

## Personality

Simple, honest, approachable — a person, not an agency machine. Practical over hype. Light humour, no jargon. Someone who explains AI the way a good GP explains a diagnosis: clearly, without over-complicating, and without making the patient feel stupid for not knowing.

## Messaging Framework

1. Symptom — what's the business struggling with (lost time, missed leads, manual admin, slow response)
2. Diagnosis — what's actually causing it
3. Prescription — the specific AI/automation fix, explained simply
4. Outcome — what changes for them once it's in place

## Visual Direction

Minimalist. Current logo is a clean, minimalist vector mark. Keep future visuals consistent with that — simple, uncluttered, no visual hype to match the no-hype voice.

## Tone Reference

See writing-style.md for full detail: professional but approachable, UK English, short paragraphs, no bullet points in client-facing writing, minimal emojis.
