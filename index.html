Digital Footprint

We diagnose your business before we prescribe anything.

Most AI companies want to sell you a tool. We'd rather find out first if you actually need one.

Digital Footprint helps small and medium businesses work out where time is genuinely being lost — and whether AI is actually the right fix, or whether something simpler would do. Sometimes it's a build. Sometimes it's a change to how a process works. Sometimes the honest answer is that nothing needs to change yet. All three are good outcomes.

That's the whole idea behind the AI Prescription: we look properly before we recommend anything, the same way a good doctor examines you before writing a prescription, not after.

If you're not sure whether this applies to a business your size, have a read of how it works, or see who this tends to help. If you're ready to talk, we can just talk.
