# Website Content Architecture

> **Layer:** Operations (working document, not source of truth) | **Purpose:** The information architecture and reasoning behind every page of dfootprint.co.uk — the canonical page-level detail, superseding the brief version in Website_Strategy.md section 3 | **Status:** Active | **Owner:** Founder | **Version:** 1.0

No final copy, slogans, or generic AI agency messaging here — this defines what each page must accomplish and why, from the visitor's actual point of view, so that copywriting (a separate, later task) has a clear brief to write against. Every page is written from the perspective of a small business owner seeking clarity, not a technology buyer.

---

## Home

**Visitor state of mind:** Arrived via a link, a LinkedIn post, or a referral, carrying some mix of quiet frustration about repetitive work and general uncertainty about whether "AI" is relevant to a business their size. Worried about being sold something they don't understand or don't need. Trying to answer: "Is this actually different from the AI marketing I've already seen and ignored?"

**Page objective:** The visitor should understand, within seconds, that this is a diagnosis service, not an AI sales pitch — and that the honest answer to their situation might be "you don't need this."

**Trust barriers to overcome:** Skepticism from prior exposure to generic AI hype marketing. Doubt that a business their size is a sensible fit for anything here.

**Key concepts to communicate:** Diagnosis comes before any recommendation. The outcome can be "no AI needed," and that's a genuinely good result. This is a real person, not a faceless agency.

**Evidence required:** None invented — if no real case studies exist yet, the page must not fabricate any; the founder's own credibility and the clarity of the explanation carry the page until real evidence exists.

**Desired next action:** Move to "How It Works" or "Who It Helps" if still deciding, or directly to "AI Prescription Assessment" / "Contact" if already convinced.

**Connection to the AI Prescription journey:** This page exists entirely before the journey begins — it's the front door, not a stage of the methodology itself.

## AI Prescription Assessment

**Visitor state of mind:** Has likely already read Home and at least one trust-building page. Now trying to answer: "What exactly am I paying for, and what actually happens to me?" Worried about hidden commitment — that booking this quietly commits them to buying a build afterward.

**Page objective:** The visitor should understand precisely what the Assessment includes, what it costs, and that the outcome is genuinely open — including "you don't need this" — before they consider booking.

**Trust barriers to overcome:** Fear that "assessment" is marketing language for a disguised sales call. Uncertainty about value for money from someone new to the market.

**Key concepts to communicate:** The Assessment is a standalone, paid diagnosis — implementation is a separate, later decision. Every outcome (build, process fix, referral, or no action) is treated as a legitimate result of the diagnosis. The client keeps the written report regardless of what they decide next.

**Evidence required:** A clear, honest price statement (even if "founding client rate, contact for details" during the pilot phase) and a plain description of what happens at each stage — no invented statistics about typical outcomes until real pilot data exists.

**Desired next action:** Book an Assessment, or move to Contact if not quite ready to commit.

**Connection to the AI Prescription journey:** This page describes the entire methodology chain from the client's side — it's the client-facing mirror of `AI_Prescription_Assessment_Specification.md` and `AI_Prescription_Client_Experience.md`.

## How It Works

**Visitor state of mind:** Curious but cautious, wanting to understand the process before committing time or money. Trying to answer: "What actually happens, step by step, and is it going to be complicated or time-consuming for me?"

**Page objective:** The visitor should be able to describe the diagnose-before-prescribe sequence back in their own words after reading — intake, conversation, analysis, report, decision — without needing any prior knowledge of AI or consulting.

**Trust barriers to overcome:** Fear of an opaque, jargon-heavy, or drawn-out process. Concern that "diagnosis" is just consulting-speak for something vague and expensive.

**Key concepts to communicate:** Every stage explained in plain terms, translated from `AI Prescription.md`'s core sequence without naming internal methodology terms (no "AI Suitability Test," no "prioritisation factors" by name — the underlying honesty is what matters, not the internal vocabulary).

**Evidence required:** None beyond a clear, accurate description of the real process — nothing here needs external proof, only clarity.

**Desired next action:** Move to "Who It Helps" or directly to "AI Prescription Assessment."

**Connection to the AI Prescription journey:** A plain-language public translation of `AI Prescription.md`'s core sequence and `11 SOP.md`'s client journey — must stay accurate to the real process, never simplified in a way that misrepresents it.

## Who It Helps

**Visitor state of mind:** Actively self-qualifying — trying to answer: "Is this actually meant for a business like mine, or am I about to waste my time and someone else's?" Often carrying a specific worry: "we're too small," "we're too old-fashioned," "we're not really a tech business."

**Page objective:** The visitor should be able to honestly assess, without needing to talk to anyone, whether they're a good fit — and feel respected either way, including if the honest answer is "probably not yet."

**Trust barriers to overcome:** The specific "this isn't for businesses like mine" objection named in `Website_Strategy.md` section 2 — must be addressed directly, not just implied through vague language.

**Key concepts to communicate:** The target profile in plain terms (drawn from `ideal_client.md`, not repeated verbatim as internal criteria but translated into recognisable, everyday descriptions). An honest note on who this probably isn't the right fit for yet, stated with the same respect as who it is for.

**Evidence required:** None fabricated — described honestly as a profile, not backed by claimed statistics about past clients until real data exists.

**Desired next action:** Move to "AI Prescription Assessment" if they recognise themselves, or leave the site without being chased if they don't.

**Connection to the AI Prescription journey:** A public-facing version of the qualification thinking in `Founder_Outreach_System.md` section 4, made visible before any conversation even starts.

## About Founder

**Visitor state of mind:** Wondering: "Who am I actually going to be dealing with — a real person, or a brand pretending to be bigger than it is?" Often reassured, not put off, by discovering it's one person, if that person is credible and relatable.

**Page objective:** The visitor should come away trusting the founder as a real, honest individual with a genuine reason to be doing this work — not evaluating credentials, but forming a personal impression.

**Trust barriers to overcome:** Distrust of anonymous-feeling agencies. Skepticism of claimed expertise with nothing personal behind it.

**Key concepts to communicate:** The founder's real background, including the non-technical starting point, framed as a strength (relatability, empathy, plain-language communication) rather than downplayed. Why this business exists — the personal motivation behind it (per `00 Vision.md`), told honestly, without over-dramatising it.

**Evidence required:** None beyond genuine, verifiable personal detail — this page's credibility comes from honesty, not from claims.

**Desired next action:** Move to "Contact" once personal trust is established, or "AI Prescription Assessment" if already convinced.

**Connection to the AI Prescription journey:** Establishes the human relationship the diagnosis will depend on — the Assessment works because the client trusts the person running it, and this page is where that trust starts forming.

## Resources

**Visitor state of mind:** Trying to verify expertise before committing money — "Does this person actually know what they're talking about, in a way I can check for myself first?"

**Page objective:** The visitor should leave with at least one genuinely useful piece of thinking, whether or not they ever book anything — real value given away, not a teaser withholding the actual insight.

**Trust barriers to overcome:** Skepticism that "expertise" claims are marketing rather than substance.

**Key concepts to communicate:** Real diagnostic thinking (what the five AI-suitability conditions mean in plain terms, how to tell a process problem from a technology problem, honest AI capability/limitation explainers) — never generic AI trend commentary, per `Market_Entry_Plan.md` section 3's explicit avoid-list.

**Evidence required:** Genuinely accurate, non-hyped explanations; real anonymised examples only once pilot data exists, never invented ones presented as real.

**Desired next action:** Return later, or move directly to "AI Prescription Assessment" if an article resolves their hesitation.

**Connection to the AI Prescription journey:** Sits entirely outside the paid journey — this is the "teach as much as build" principle from `00 Vision.md` made visible publicly, independent of whether it converts anyone.

## Contact

**Visitor state of mind:** Ready for some form of human contact but wary of what that commits them to — "If I reach out, am I about to be chased by a salesperson?"

**Page objective:** The visitor should feel that reaching out is genuinely low-commitment and low-risk — a conversation, not a sales process starting.

**Trust barriers to overcome:** Fear of aggressive follow-up once contact information is given.

**Key concepts to communicate:** What happens after they submit the form (a real, personal reply from the founder, not an automated sales sequence). That reaching out doesn't commit them to booking or buying anything.

**Evidence required:** None — this page's job is reassurance about process, not persuasion.

**Desired next action:** Form submission, per `Website_Build_Specification.md` section 7.

**Connection to the AI Prescription journey:** The literal starting point of the real journey — everything before this page exists to make this single step feel safe.

## Architecture-Wide Principles

Educate before asking for commitment — every page above builds Understanding or Trust (per `Website_Strategy.md` section 5) before any page asks for a booking or payment; only the Assessment and Contact pages carry a primary commercial call to action, and both are reached, by design, after at least one trust-building page. The Assessment is never framed as a funnel toward an AI sale — every page describing it states the open-outcome nature of the diagnosis explicitly. "No AI needed" is treated as a positive, stated outcome on Home and the Assessment page specifically, not confined to fine print. No page leads with technology — every page's key concepts are written business-problem-first, with AI mentioned only once the underlying idea (honest diagnosis, proportionate fix) has been established.
