# Validation Dashboard

> **Layer:** Operations (working document, not source of truth) | **Purpose:** Metrics tracked during the Phase 1 (10 Roadmap.md) 30-day validation window | **Status:** Active — structure ready, no data yet | **Owner:** Founder | **Version:** 1.0

This is a tracking structure, not a live report — it contains no invented or placeholder findings. Fill in as real pilot conversations happen. Update after every conversation, per Post_Conversation_Review_Process.md. Row-level, day-to-day logging happens in `Pilot_Execution_Dashboard.md`; the figures below are the weekly rollup of that log, per its Weekly Review Structure.

## Pipeline Metrics

Conversations initiated (count). Pre-qualifying conversations completed. Businesses matching `ideal_client.md` criteria. Assessments booked. Assessments completed. Target: 5 completed Assessments within 30 days (per `10 Roadmap.md` Phase 1).

## Conversion Metrics

Pre-qualifying conversations → booked Assessments (rate). Booked → completed (rate, tracks drop-off/no-shows). Assessments completed → paid without negotiation (per the success signals in `10 Roadmap.md`).

## Client Reaction Metrics

For each completed Assessment: did the client ask about next steps unprompted (yes/no)? Did the client say they'd refer someone else (yes/no)? Reaction to any "don't build this" recommendation — logged as relief/interest, neutral, or disappointment, with a brief note (this is the single most important signal for testing the diagnosis-first philosophy, not a side metric).

## Process Metrics

Actual time spent per stage (Health Check review, session, analysis, report writing, debrief) versus the estimates in `11 SOP.md`. Any SOP or template step flagged as awkward or unclear during use (cross-reference `13 Lessons Learned.md` entries).

## Evidence Metrics

Number of completed Client Conversation Capture Template records. Number of one-off observations logged (per Post_Conversation_Review_Process.md Step 2). Number of findings that reached repeated-pattern status (Step 3) and were entered into Lessons Learned. Number of Lessons Learned entries, if any, that reach the review-point promotion threshold.

## Review Point Summary (completed at end of 30 days or after 5 Assessments, whichever first)

Overall read on the three success signals from `10 Roadmap.md`: payment friction, unprompted next-step interest, and reaction to "no AI needed" findings. Recommended decision: proceed to Phase 2, adjust and re-run a second small round, or pause and reassess — decided using the evidence above, not a general impression.
