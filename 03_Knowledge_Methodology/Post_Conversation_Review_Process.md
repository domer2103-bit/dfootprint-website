# Post-Conversation Review Process

> **Layer:** Knowledge & Methodology | **Purpose:** Decides where a completed conversation capture's content should live — Client Record, Lessons Learned, or a one-off note | **Status:** Active — source of truth | **Owner:** Founder | **Version:** 1.0

Run this immediately after every real conversation, once the Client Conversation Capture Template is filled in. This process routes information to the right place under Governance.md's one-fact-one-file rule — nothing gets duplicated across destinations.

## Step 1: Client Record

If the conversation relates to a specific, named (or anonymised) real client or prospective client, everything client-specific goes into their own copy of `Client_Template.md` in `05_Client_Records/` — the completed Business DNA fields, the diagnosis findings, the specific quotes and evidence. This is always done, for every real conversation with an identifiable business, regardless of whether anything else below also applies.

## Step 2: Is This a One-Off Observation?

Ask: has this exact friction, reaction, or finding appeared in only this one conversation so far? If yes, it stays as a one-off observation — logged only inside that client's record, not elsewhere. It is explicitly not entered into `13 Lessons Learned.md` yet. One-off observations are not ignored; they are watched. Keep a simple running note (in the Client Record or a lightweight tracking list) of what to look for in future conversations.

## Step 3: Is This a Repeat?

Before logging anything as a Lessons Learned entry, check whether the same finding has already appeared as a one-off observation in a previous conversation, using the Evidence Extraction Framework's repetition test (same underlying friction, independently reported, at least two separate businesses). If yes, this qualifies as a genuine repeated pattern.

## Step 4: Create the Lessons Learned Entry

Only repeated patterns (per Step 3) get entered into `13 Lessons Learned.md`. Each entry should state: what pattern was observed, which conversations/client records it draws from (by reference), what was expected going in, and what actually happened. This is a raw entry — it is not yet a conclusion about what should change in the methodology.

## Step 5: Does This Deserve Promotion Into the Operating System?

Per Governance.md section 4, a Lessons Learned entry only gets promoted into a permanent source-of-truth document (AI Prescription.md, SOP, Automation Catalog, Company Philosophy, or a template) at a scheduled review point, not immediately, and only when the pattern has repeated enough to be trusted — per the standing principle already established: "No changes to methodology from single feedback. Repeated patterns create improvements." During the 30-day validation window (see `10 Roadmap.md` Phase 1), the review point is the end-of-pilot review, not each individual conversation.

## Quick Reference

One conversation, new finding → Client Record only, watched, not logged to Lessons Learned. Same finding appears a second time, independently → Lessons Learned entry created. Lessons Learned entry confirmed as a real repeated pattern at a review point → candidate for promotion into a named source-of-truth document, via Governance.md's normal process.
