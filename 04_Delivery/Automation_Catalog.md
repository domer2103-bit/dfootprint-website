# Automation Catalog

> **Layer:** Delivery | **Purpose:** Productised catalogue of reusable AI solutions | **Status:** Active — source of truth | **Owner:** Founder | **Version:** 1.0

Not "I build automations" — "I have products." Each entry below is a repeatable, productised solution rather than a bespoke build from scratch. Every client engagement should start by checking this catalogue first: can an existing product be adapted, before we design something new. This is how the business scales without trading more hours for more clients.

Each product should eventually have its own build spec — architecture, tools used, setup time, typical ROI range — once it's been delivered at least once.

## AI Receptionist

Answers and routes incoming calls or messages. Handles common questions, takes messages, escalates when needed. Maps to the Phone Calls and Email Overload problem areas.

## AI Appointment Booking

Lets customers book, reschedule, or cancel appointments without a human involved. Syncs with existing calendar/booking software. Maps to Appointment Booking and Scheduling.

## AI Lead Qualification

Reviews incoming leads and sorts them by fit and intent before a human spends time on them. Maps to Lead Follow-up.

## AI Follow-up

Automatically follows up with leads or customers who've gone quiet, at the right intervals, in the business's tone of voice. Maps to Lead Follow-up.

## AI Proposal Generator

Turns a conversation or brief set of inputs into a formatted quote or proposal automatically. Maps to Quotations.

## AI CRM Assistant

Keeps CRM records updated automatically from calls, emails, or forms, instead of relying on manual entry. Maps to Data Entry and Reporting.

## AI Review Assistant

Requests reviews at the right moment, and drafts responses to incoming reviews for approval. Maps to Review Management.

## AI Content Engine

Turns a business's existing knowledge or activity into a steady stream of social media and marketing content. Maps to Social Media.

## AI Internal Knowledge Assistant

A searchable assistant trained on a business's internal documentation, so staff can ask questions instead of digging through files. Maps to Internal Documentation, Knowledge Management, and Staff Onboarding.

## AI Email Assistant

Triages, drafts, and organises inbox email so less time is spent reading and typing. Maps to Email Overload.

## Notes

This list will grow as new problems come up with real clients. When a bespoke solution gets built for one client and could reasonably serve another business with a similar problem, add it here as a new product rather than treating it as a one-off. Cross-reference each product against Business_Problems.md so every catalogue entry maps clearly to the pain it solves.
