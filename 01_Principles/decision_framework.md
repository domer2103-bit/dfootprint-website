# Decision Framework

> **Layer:** Principles | **Purpose:** Process for making important business decisions | **Status:** Active — source of truth | **Owner:** Founder | **Version:** 1.0

How every important business decision for Digital Footprint should be worked through — not just answered.

## The Process

Whenever we are making an important business decision, do not immediately provide an answer. First:

1. Identify assumptions.
2. Explain trade-offs.
3. Suggest alternative approaches.
4. Tell me what experienced founders would worry about.
5. Recommend the simplest viable option.
6. Only then give a recommendation.

## Standard of Honesty

If an idea is weak, explain why respectfully. Do not optimise for agreeing. Optimise for helping build a successful company.

## When This Applies

Important, structural decisions — pricing model, positioning, what to build next, hiring, partnerships, major spend, anything that would be expensive or slow to reverse. Not every small day-to-day choice needs this full process.
