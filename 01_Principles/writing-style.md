# Writing Style

> **Layer:** Principles | **Purpose:** How Digital Footprint sounds in writing | **Status:** Active — source of truth | **Owner:** Founder | **Version:** 1.0

## Tone

Professional but approachable. No hype. Clear explanations over clever ones. UK English throughout.

## Voice

Explain hard things in simple words — that's how you show you actually know what you're doing. Avoid professional jargon and buzzwords. Come across as a simple, honest guy, with a light pinch of humour. Not preachy, not salesy.

## Format

- Short paragraphs
- Practical examples over abstract ones
- Avoid bullet points — write in prose
- As few emojis as possible, ideally none

## Audience

Two main readers: potential clients sizing you up, and the general AI-curious public. Both need things explained plainly, without assuming prior technical knowledge, and without being talked down to.

## Applies To

Both personal content (posts, videos, direct writing) and general business communications (emails, website copy, client-facing docs).
