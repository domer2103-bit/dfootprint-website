Who this is for

Somewhere between about 5 and 50 people. An owner who's still properly involved in the day-to-day, not several layers removed from how things actually run. No dedicated IT department — you're the one who ends up dealing with technology questions, on top of everything else. Something repetitive or time-consuming that's been bothering you for a while, even if you can't quite put your finger on the fix.

You don't need to be "a tech business." Most of the businesses this suits best aren't. You just need to be curious enough to want a proper answer, rather than another guess.

Who this probably isn't for yet

If you're looking for a specific tool to be built without any diagnosis first, this isn't that — the Assessment always comes before any build. And if there's genuinely nothing currently costing you time or stress, save your money, and maybe come back if that changes.
